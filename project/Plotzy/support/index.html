At a glance

Tell us what happened, and where.

The fastest support request includes your iOS version, Plotzy version, the feature you were using, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not include passwords, session tokens, or private information.

  • Reply by email

01Start here

For in-app help, open Profile → Help center in Plotzy. Your email app will open a message addressed to our technical team.

Before writing, try closing and reopening Plotzy, checking your connection, and installing any available app or iOS update. If the issue remains, tell us what you expected to happen and what you saw instead.

  • Include: Plotzy version, iOS version, device model, feature name, and approximate time of the issue.
  • Describe: the steps to reproduce the problem and whether it happens every time or only on one story.
  • Protect: remove passwords, session tokens, payment details, and other information that support does not need.

02Playback and Listen

If a video does not start, check that your connection is stable, then tap Retry. A signed playback source can take a moment to load after a network change. The episode remains selected while you retry.

Use Listen to keep the same episode and position while the video surface is hidden. Choose Watch to restore the video. If audio stops in the background, check that Silent mode, Focus, and your device output route allow audio playback, then reopen Plotzy and try again.

03Favorites and History

Favorites and History belong to the guest session on your device. Keep Plotzy installed and connected to the internet while a save or playback record is syncing. A story can remain visible while the request is being retried.

If a saved story or progress marker looks wrong, include the story title, episode number, and the action you took. Do not send a visitor identifier or session token; the app can provide the relevant context through Help center.

04AI Writing

AI Writing needs a network connection to generate an outline, chapters, and screenplay sections. Keep the app open while a request is preparing, and retry from the same stage if a request fails.

Saved writing remains available for editing and export. If generation stops, tell us the project stage, the last action you selected, and whether the project opens normally. Do not include private story text unless you want the team to review it.
