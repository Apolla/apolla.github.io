At a glance

Enjoy stories without sharing your identity.

Plotzy does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, or email address to watch. Essential guest-session and viewing data support the app’s features. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising profiles.

  • No personal sign-up
  • No sale of your data
  • Support on your terms

01Personal information

Plotzy is operated by its individual owner (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains information handled by the app and these legal pages.

You can use Plotzy without entering your real name, phone number, email address, or other registration details. The app uses a guest session so you can start exploring stories.

We do not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location for the app’s current features. If you choose to email us, we receive the information you include in that message, as described below.

02Data that makes the app work

Some information is needed to provide playback and the features you choose to use:

  • Guest session. A randomly generated visitor identifier and a session token recognize your app installation and keep your guest session working. The visitor identifier is sent to our service with app requests.
  • Basic technical information. App version, platform, language, and a region setting accompany service requests. These values help the service return the appropriate content.
  • Saved stories and viewing history. When you save a drama or play an episode, the relevant drama or episode identifier is sent to the service. Favorites and playback records are associated with your guest session to support saved stories and viewing history.

These records support the service. They are not used by Plotzy to build advertising profiles.

03Storage on your device

Plotzy stores your guest identifier, session token, app preferences, saved stories, and viewing progress on your device. Artwork and video may be cached to improve loading and playback.

To remove cached media, open Profile → Clear cache in the app. Clearing the cache does not delete your guest session, favorites, local viewing progress, or server-side viewing history.

These legal pages do not set cookies, load advertising tools, or run analytics scripts.

04When you contact us

Contact is optional. To email our technical team, open Profile → Help center in Plotzy and send a message using your email app.

We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Your email provider handles delivery under its own privacy policy.

05Service providers and sharing

Hosting, content delivery, and playback services process the technical information needed to deliver the app and its video content. When your device connects to an online service, that service also receives connection information such as your IP address.

We do not sell or rent your information. Information is used to operate and support Plotzy, and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

06Your choices and questions

You can choose whether to save stories and whether to contact support. You can remove a saved story using the app’s favorite control and clear cached media through Profile.

For questions about guest-session records, access to information, or a request to delete information associated with your use of Plotzy, email our technical team through Profile → Help center. We may need enough information to locate the relevant guest session and verify the request.

Guest-session, favorite, and playback records support the service; support messages support your request. Retention and deletion may depend on the type of record, the need to operate the service, and applicable legal requirements. Contact us for help with a specific record.

07Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when Plotzy’s features or information practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version. Any material change will be communicated as required by applicable law.

Our technical team can help. Open Help center inside Plotzy to compose an email with your question or request, or visit the technical support page for troubleshooting steps.
